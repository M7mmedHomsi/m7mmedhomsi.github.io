Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This policy explains how Jebbeh Group ("we", "us") handles data in its internal content automation tool ("the app").

What the app does

The app is used internally by Jebbeh Group to prepare and upload videos to Jebbeh Group's own YouTube channel and to keep a record of processed videos in a Google Sheets spreadsheet. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

With the account owner's permission, the app accesses:

  • YouTube: to upload videos and set their titles and descriptions on Jebbeh Group's channel.
  • Google Sheets: to read and update a tracking spreadsheet of video IDs and upload status.

How we use the data

Data is used only to perform the functions described above. We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Jebbeh Group's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data storage and retention

Access tokens are stored securely within our automation platform and are used only to run the app. Video IDs and upload status are kept in our internal spreadsheet for record-keeping.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact jebbehgroup.ma@gmail.com.
